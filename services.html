FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to what most founders ask first.

1

Working together

What kind of projects do you take on?
Brand strategy, visual identity and packaging for founder-led D2C businesses. If your product is made and you are not sure what it says yet, that is the work.
Do you take on logo-only projects?
Rarely, and I will say so honestly. A logo with no positioning behind it usually gets replaced within two years. I would rather do a smaller piece of work that starts with the decisions.
How involved will I be?
More than you expect at the start, less than you fear later. The first stage needs your time. After that it is scheduled check-ins and clear feedback rounds.
2

Money and time

How much does a project cost?
Every package is a starting point. We shape the scope around what you need, then fix it. The price is set before we start and never changes after.
How do you handle revisions?
Three rounds of changes are included. Feedback is collected in one place and worked through together, not traded over email.
Will I own the final files?
Yes. On final payment, full ownership of the delivered work transfers to you, including editable source files.
