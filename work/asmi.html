For Shruti Patki

Asmi

Brand Strategy/Visual Identity/Packaging Design

Asmi is an online clothing brand that creates printed t-shirts and hoodies for teenagers and young adults who value self-expression.

Client
Shruti Patki
Year
2026
Sector
Clothing
Services
Brand Strategy, Visual Identity, Packaging Design

The brand

Rooted in a blend of cultural symbolism and modern design, the brand brings together meaningful storytelling with contemporary aesthetics. The identity draws inspiration from Indian heritage, most notably through the Devanagari “अ” and Lord Krishna’s flute, while presenting it in a way that feels fresh, relatable, and expressive for today’s generation. Asmi stands as a celebration of individuality, creativity, and cultural pride.

Bridging culture with contemporary expression

The visual identity for Asmi was crafted to merge tradition with modern design sensibilities. By integrating symbolic elements like Krishna’s flute and organic leaf forms into a minimal and adaptable system, the brand creates a distinctive visual language that feels both rooted and current. Supporting assets, patterns, and typography extend this identity across touchpoints, ensuring consistency while allowing flexibility. Every design decision reflects the brand’s core values of empowerment, inclusivity, and authenticity while appealing to a digitally native audience.

A distinctive identity with meaning at its core

Asmi’s brand identity goes beyond aesthetics. It tells a story. By combining cultural depth with modern simplicity, the brand creates a unique space in the streetwear market. The result is a cohesive and versatile system that stands out visually and resonates emotionally with its audience, making Asmi more than just a clothing label. It becomes a medium of self-expression.
