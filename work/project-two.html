The brief

Who came to you, what stage the business was at, and what was getting in their way. Keep it concrete. The reader is deciding whether their own problem sounds like this one.

The decision

Name the one decision that made this project work. The audience you found, the position nobody else was holding, the thing the founder had been saying without realising it. The work is described by decisions, not adjectives.

The outcome

What changed. Ideally what the brand could then charge, or who it could then talk to. A number is better than an adjective.
