Project editor

 

Projects

    This page edits a copy in your browser. It cannot save by itself. When you are done press Download projects.json, put the file in content/ replacing the old one, then run node build/build.js and push. Empty fields are simply left off the live site.

    Testimonials

    Leave a quote empty and its card shows on the site as a blank slot. Fill one in and it becomes a real testimonial. Only use words a client actually said, with their permission.

    Raw JSON

    If the editor cannot reach the file, paste the contents of content/projects.json here and press Load.
