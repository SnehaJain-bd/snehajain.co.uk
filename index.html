Hello, I am Sneha

A graphic designer and brand strategist with a soft spot for brands that have something honest to say.

I work with independent founders, studios and in-house teams across food, culture and the built environment — the kind of projects where the brief arrives half-formed and the real work is figuring out what the business is trying to become.

My background sits on both sides of the table: the strategic thinking that decides why a brand exists, and the hands-on craft that decides how it looks and feels. That means fewer handovers, less lost in translation, and work that stays true to the idea it started from.

Outside of client work you will find me collecting type specimens, over-photographing packaging in supermarkets, and redesigning menus in my head.
