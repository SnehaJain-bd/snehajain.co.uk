P3, with the deliverables

You are right that a founder cannot judge a package without knowing what arrives. Same card in all four, only the deliverables treatment changes.

The deliverables below are mine, not yours. I have written plausible ones from your service descriptions so the layouts can be judged at real length. Every one of them needs replacing with what you actually hand over, and none of it should go live until you have written it.

Your drawn tick, four times per card. It reads as a promise rather than a spec, and it puts one of your marks somewhere it genuinely means something. The risk is repetition: sixteen ticks on one page is a lot of the same shape.
