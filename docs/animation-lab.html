Animation choices

Words for the things that move, and five different heroes to watch. Pick what you like, then tell me in these terms.

How to use this

You do not need the vocabulary before you start. Watch first, react second. Every demo below has a Play button and you can press it as often as you like.

  • Part one breaks motion into five separate dials. Each one is shown on its own so you can see what it does by itself.
  • Part two is your real hero, five different ways, using your actual words.
  • Part three lists the other places on the site where motion could go, so you can say yes or no to each.

If your system is set to reduce motion, the demos still play when you press the button, because you asked for them. The site itself stays completely still for anyone with that setting on, which is deliberate and will not change.

Part one: the five dials

Almost any note you might want to give me is one of these five turned up or down.

1. Distance

How far a thing travels before it settles. Short reads as crisp and confident. Long reads as dramatic, and starts to feel slow even at the same speed.

8px
20px
48px

Say: travels further, or barely moves.

2. Speed

How long it takes. Under a quarter second feels snappy and almost invisible. Over a second starts to feel like the page is keeping you waiting.

0.25s
0.60s
1.20s

Say: faster, slower, or it drags.

3. Easing

The character of the speed, and the one that changes the feel the most. All four of these travel the same distance in the same time. They do not feel remotely alike.

even
gentle
decisive
overshoot

Even is machinery, and almost always wrong. Gentle is what the site uses now. Decisive leaves fast and lands softly, which reads as assured. Overshoot goes slightly too far and comes back, which reads as playful.

Say: more decisive, give it some bounce, or too bouncy.

4. Stagger

The gap between one thing arriving and the next. This is what makes a row of cards feel like a considered sequence rather than a single lump.

tight
normal
loose

Say: let them arrive closer together, or one at a time, more separated.

Part three: everywhere else

The hero is one moment. These are the others, most of which are currently doing nothing or doing the same fade as everything else. Say yes or no to each.

  • Work cards. On hover, the image could push in slightly while the title shifts, so the card feels like a physical thing being picked up. Currently it only changes colour.
  • Case study covers. Already drift slightly as they pass. Could be made stronger or removed.
  • Section headings. Currently every heading on the site fades up identically. The yellow highlighter could draw itself on the key phrase of each one instead, the way the hero does in C.
  • Numbers and counts. Figures like "twenty plus clients" could count up as they come into view. Effective once, irritating if used more than once on a page.
  • Buttons. Currently a colour change. The arrow could travel, or the fill could wipe across rather than switch.
  • Between pages. At the moment clicking a link is an instant cut. A short crossfade or a wipe in your off-white would make the site feel like one continuous thing rather than separate documents.
  • The approach section. The steps could draw a line connecting them as you scroll, which suits content that is genuinely a sequence.
  • Scroll-linked headline. A large phrase that shifts slowly as you scroll past it, giving the page depth without anything blinking or popping.

How to tell me

Anything in this form is enough to act on. You do not need to be more precise than this.

  • "Hero B, but more decisive and a bit faster."
  • "C, and put the marker on building instead."
  • "E, but the wash is too strong, and I do not want the block to scale."
  • "D is too much. B everywhere, and yes to the work cards and page transitions, no to counting numbers."
  • "Everything is too slow across the whole site."

If none of the five is right, say what is wrong with the closest one. "B but it still feels flat" is more useful than a blank page, and I can take it from there.
